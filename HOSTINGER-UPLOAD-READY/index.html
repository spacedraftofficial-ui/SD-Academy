Our Learning Framework

Foundation. Discipline. Elevation.

Every SD Group employee progresses through three intentional stages of growth.

01

Foundation

Company induction, safety, code of conduct, systems access and role basics. Every new employee starts here.

  • Company & group induction
  • EHS and safety basics
  • HR policies & conduct
  • Systems and tools orientation
02

Discipline

SOP compliance, role-based assessments, structured review cadence and operational accountability.

  • SOP & checklist adherence
  • Role assessments & validation
  • Manager review cycle
  • Mandatory compliance training
03

Elevation

Advanced skills, leadership development, external certifications and career-level progression.

  • Leadership & management skills
  • Advanced technical training
  • Certification programmes
  • Career-path planning
How It Works

The Employee Learning Journey

From your first day to your next career milestone — a governed, transparent process.

1

Account Created

HR creates your account after joining formalities are complete.

2

Company & Department Assigned

Your entity, division and department are mapped to your profile.

3

Role & Level Mapped

Your job role and career level determine the learning curriculum assigned.

4

Mandatory Onboarding Released

Group and company induction modules are automatically assigned with due dates.

5

Learning Path Assigned

Role-based and elective modules are released based on your department and level.

6

Assessments Completed

Quizzes, practical tasks and SOP acknowledgments must be completed to progress.

7

Manager Validation

Your reporting manager reviews and approves your practical assignments where required.

8

Certificate Issued

Verified SD Group certificates are issued on successful completion of all criteria.

9

Career Progression Updated

Your development record is updated, informing your next learning path and role review.

About SD Academy

The SD Group Employee Learning Platform

SD Academy is the SD Group's controlled system for employee onboarding, role readiness, SOP knowledge, assessment, certification and career development. It is not a generic course website — it is the official, governed learning platform for every SD Group employee, from intern to director.

Every piece of content on SD Academy is mapped to a company, department and role. Every certificate is issued only after passing an assessment. Every SOP is version-controlled with an owner, approver and review date.
